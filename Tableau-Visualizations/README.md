# 📊 Tableau  Project Portfolio

This section showcases 5 data analytics dashboards across business, finance, HR, and aviation domains. Projects were created using Tableau and Power BI to drive decisions through data.

---

## 🔹 Project 1: Sales & Customer Dashboard (Tableau)
**Description**: Visualized sales performance and customer insights to support business growth.  
✅ Customer trends: Total customers, avg. order value, and order volume  
✅ Revenue drivers: Subcategories and seasonal trends  
✅ Profitability: Product category margins and growth  
🔗 [View Live Dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/egbe.grace/viz/SalesCustomerDashboardsDynamic_17385906491570/CustomerDashboard)

---

## 🔹 Project 2: Sales Performance Dashboard (Tableau)
**Description**: Explored year-over-year performance, segment behavior, and regional opportunities.  
✅ Sales grew 20.36% YoY; profit dropped 14.24% → cost optimization needed  
✅ Region insights: West performed best; flagged underperforming teams  
✅ Segment split: Consumer (50.23%), Corporate (44.54%), Home Office (29.71%)

---

## 🔹 Project 3: Financial Loan Dashboard (Tableau)
**Description**: Assessed loan health and funding quality across 39,000+ loans.  
✅ 85.83% loans classified as good, $368M+ funded  
✅ Risk: Small business and debt consolidation had high default  
✅ Region: California, TX, NY lead in loan volume  
📌 Tools: Tableau + Excel  
📌 Skills: Risk segmentation, geo-visuals, financial KPIs

---

## 🔹 Project 4: HR Analytics Dashboard (Power BI)
**Description**: Monitored attrition, employee demographics, and job satisfaction.  
✅ 16.12% attrition rate; R&D team highest at 56%  
✅ Gender gap: Male attrition higher (63%)  
✅ Job Satisfaction Heatmap revealed at-risk departments  
📌 Tools: Power BI + Excel  
📌 Skills: DAX, custom visuals, segmentation analysis

---

## 🔹 Project 5: Aviation Accident Dashboard (Power BI)
**Description**: Visualized 88,000+ aviation accidents by location, flight phase, and severity.  
✅ 92.5% of incidents in the U.S., most in Anchorage  
✅ Risky phases: Landing (17%), Takeoff (14%), Cruise (11%)  
✅ 72% incidents led to substantial aircraft damage  
📌 Tools: Power BI, DAX  
📌 Skills: Aggregation, root cause analysis, safety analytics
